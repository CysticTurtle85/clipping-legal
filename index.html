Turn long videos into short clips, and post them to TikTok

Clip Brain is a desktop app for creators. It finds the strongest moments in long videos, edits them into vertical clips with captions, and posts the ones you approve to your own TikTok account.

See how it works

How it works

  1. Add your sources. Point Clip Brain at the long-form videos and streams you have the rights to clip.
  2. It finds the moments. Clip Brain transcribes each video and picks moments that stand on their own, with a strong first line.
  3. It edits the clip. Each moment becomes a 1080×1920 vertical video with burned-in captions, a title and a cover.
  4. You review it. Watch each clip, trim the start or end, choose its sound, or skip it. Nothing is posted until you approve it.
  5. It posts to your TikTok. Connect your account with TikTok's own login. You choose who can view each video and its comment, duet and stitch settings, and Clip Brain posts it, or sends it to your TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the app.

Built for TikTok

Uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Every post shows your account, a preview, the caption and your privacy choice before it goes out.

Your results in one place

See each post's views, likes, comments and shares, and which posting times work best for your account.

Posting windows

Plans when each clip should go up, and reminds you when a window opens.

Your data stays yours

Runs on your own computer. Tokens are stored locally and you can disconnect an account at any time, which revokes its access with TikTok.

Who it's for

Creators and small teams who make short-form clips from their own long-form videos, podcasts and streams, or from creators who allow clipping, and want to post consistently without editing every clip by hand.
