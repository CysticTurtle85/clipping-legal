Clipper – Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Clipper is a personal tool that its owner uses to edit short videos and send them to the owner's own TikTok accounts as drafts. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody else can sign up for it.

Use

  • Only the owner's own TikTok accounts connect to Clipper, through TikTok's official login.
  • Clipper only uploads videos to the connected account's TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published until the account owner reviews and posts it in the TikTok app.
  • Use of Clipper must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

No warranty

Clipper is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.
