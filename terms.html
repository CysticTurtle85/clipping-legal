Last updated: 2 November 2026

These terms cover your use of Clip Brain ("the service"), a tool that makes short clips from long videos and posts them to your TikTok account when you choose to.

1. Your account and content

You must be allowed to use TikTok and to post the content you post. You are responsible for the clips you post, including having the rights to the source video, music and sounds in them, and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

2. Connecting TikTok

You connect a TikTok account through TikTok's own login page. Clip Brain asks only for the permissions it needs: your basic profile (to show which account is connected), uploading and posting videos (to post the clips you confirm), and your public video list (to show how your posts perform). You can disconnect at any time from Clip Brain's page, or remove Clip Brain in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

3. Posting

Clip Brain never posts without your confirmation. For each post you choose who can view the video, whether others may comment, Duet or Stitch it, and whether it is commercial content; you can save these choices for an account and confirm later posts with one tap. TikTok may take a few minutes to process a post, and TikTok decides whether content is published.

4. Commercial content

If a video promotes your own business or a third party, you must disclose it when posting. Branded content must follow TikTok's Branded Content Policy; by posting you also agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.

The service is provided as is, without warranties. It may change or stop at any time. We are not liable for content removed by TikTok, lost views or account actions taken by TikTok.
