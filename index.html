Clipper

Clipper turns long-form videos into short vertical clips and helps creators post them to TikTok.

  1. Clip. Clipper finds the best moments in long videos and edits them into short vertical clips with captions.
  2. Review. You watch each clip, trim it and pick its sound before anything is posted.
  3. Post. You connect your TikTok account with TikTok's own login, choose who can view the video and its settings, and Clipper posts it to your account. You can disconnect at any time.

Clipper uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. It never posts without your confirmation.
