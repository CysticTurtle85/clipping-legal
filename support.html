Support

Connecting TikTok

Open Clip Brain, choose Connect a TikTok account and log in on TikTok's page. TikTok shows exactly which permissions Clip Brain asks for before you approve.

Disconnecting

Choose Disconnect next to the account in Clip Brain: its access is revoked with TikTok and its token deleted. You can also remove Clip Brain in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

Is anything posted without me?

No. Every post needs your confirmation, with the privacy setting you choose.

Deleting your data

Ask through the contact link below and everything stored for your account is deleted. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/CysticTurtle85/clipping-legal/issues. We reply within two working days.
