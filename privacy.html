Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what Clip Brain collects when you connect a TikTok account, how it is used, and how to remove it.

What we collect from TikTok

  • Account connection: your TikTok open ID and display name (user.info.basic), so we know which account is connected.
  • Tokens: the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when you approve Clip Brain. They are stored only on the computer that runs Clip Brain, never shared, and used only to post the videos you confirm and read your own videos' public stats.
  • Your videos' stats: views, likes, comments and shares of your public videos (video.list), used to show how your posts perform and to suggest posting times.
  • Posting settings: the privacy, interaction and disclosure choices you make when posting, and any choices you save for an account.

How we use it

Only to post the clips you confirm to your own account (video.upload, video.publish), check each post's status, and show you your results. We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, or post anything you did not confirm.

Retention

Tokens and settings are kept while the account is connected. Video stats are kept to compare your posts over time and are deleted with the account's data on request. Clips are deleted after they are posted or skipped.

Disconnect and deletion

Disconnect any account from Clip Brain's page: its token is revoked with TikTok and deleted. You can also remove Clip Brain in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. To delete everything stored for your account, email github.com/CysticTurtle85/clipping-legal/issues.

Security

Data is stored on the operator's own computer, not on shared servers, and is only transmitted to TikTok over HTTPS.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
